🔴 What Is a Stranger?

A stranger is someone I do not know.

A stranger might be:

  • ❓ Someone I have never met
  • 🛒 Someone at a store I do not know
  • 🏞️ Someone at the park I do not know
  • 🚶 Someone walking nearby that I do not know

A stranger can look friendly.

A stranger can look different from me.

I cannot tell if someone is safe just by looking at them.

🔐 Keep Private Things Private

I do not need to tell strangers private information.

I keep these things private:

  • 🏠 My home address
  • 📞 My phone number
  • 🔑 My passwords
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Private family information

🎮 Tell or Keep Private?

A stranger asks for my address.

A stranger asks for my password.

A cashier says, "Have a nice day!"

🚶 I Stay With Trusted Adults

I do not go somewhere with a stranger.

If a stranger asks me to go with them, I can:

  • 🛑 Say "No."
  • 🚶 Move away.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Go to a trusted adult.
  • 💬 Tell the trusted adult.
