🔴 What Is a Stranger?

A stranger is someone I do not know.

A stranger might be:

  • Someone I see at a store but have never met
  • Someone walking through my neighborhood who I do not know
  • Someone sitting near me at an event
  • Someone who talks to me but I have never met before

A stranger may look friendly, serious, young, old, or anything in between.

I cannot tell whether someone is safe just by looking at them.

🔐 Private Information

Some information should stay private around strangers.

I do not need to tell a stranger:

  • 🏠 My home address
  • 📞 My phone number
  • 🔑 My passwords
  • 🏫 Details about where I will be alone
  • 💳 Private family information

It is okay to keep private information private.

🚶 If a Stranger Asks Me to Go Somewhere

I do not go somewhere with a stranger without permission from a trusted adult.

If a stranger asks me to go with them, I can:

  • 🛑 Say no
  • 🚶 Move away
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Go to a trusted adult
  • 💬 Tell the trusted adult what happened
