🏠 Who Can Be Family?

Every family is different!

A family might include:

  • Parents
  • Grandparents
  • Brothers and sisters
  • Stepfamily members
  • Foster family members
  • Aunts and uncles
  • Cousins
  • Guardians or caregivers

Some family members live together, and some live in different homes.

There is no one way a family has to look.
