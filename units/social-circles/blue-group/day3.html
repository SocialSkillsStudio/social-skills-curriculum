🟢 Who Is a Friend?

A friend is someone I know and have a friendship with.

Friends might:

  • 🎮 Play games together
  • 💬 Talk to each other
  • 😂 Laugh together
  • ⚽ Do activities together
  • 🤝 Help each other
  • 😊 Enjoy spending time together

A person is not automatically my friend just because they are my age or go to my school.

Friendships develop when people get to know each other.

⭐ Being a Good Friend

Friendship goes both ways.

Good friends can:

  • 👂 Listen to each other
  • 🎲 Take turns
  • 💬 Talk kindly
  • 🤝 Help each other
  • 😊 Respect each other's choices
  • ❤️ Care about each other's feelings

Friends do not have to agree about everything.

🤗 Friends and Personal Space

Friends may show that they care in different ways.

Friends might:

  • 🙌 High-five
  • 👋 Wave
  • 😊 Smile
  • 🎮 Play together
  • 💬 Talk together
  • 🤗 Hug if both people want to

Being someone's friend does not mean I have to hug them.

I can decide if I want a hug.

My friend can decide if they want a hug too.

We respect each other's personal space.
