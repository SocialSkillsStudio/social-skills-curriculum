🟡 Who Is in My Yellow Circle?

Yellow Circle people are people I know, but I may not know them as well as family or friends.

They might include:

  • 👩‍🏫 Teachers
  • 🏫 School staff
  • 🏀 Coaches
  • 🏠 Neighbors I know
  • 🎵 Instructors
  • 🚌 A bus driver I know
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Adults my family knows

I recognize these people and may talk to them, but they are not automatically part of my family or friend circles.

🤝 The Handshake Circle

We usually use more personal space with people in our Yellow Circle than we do with family.

Ways I might greet someone in my Yellow Circle include:

  • 🤝 Handshake
  • 🙌 High-five
  • 👋 Wave
  • 😊 Smile
  • 💬 Say "Hello!"

I usually do not cuddle with people in my Yellow Circle.

Different people may have different boundaries. I should respect their personal space, and they should respect mine.
