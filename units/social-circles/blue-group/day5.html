🟠 Who Is in the Orange Circle?

Orange Circle people are people I may recognize, see sometimes, or know a little.

They might include:

  • 🛒 A store worker I see often
  • 🏘️ A neighbor I recognize
  • 📚 Someone who works at the library
  • 🏥 A receptionist at a doctor's office
  • 🍎 A cafeteria worker
  • 🏫 Someone who works at my school but I do not know well

I can be friendly with these people, but I usually use more personal space.

👋 The Wave Circle

A wave is a good way to greet many people in the Orange Circle.

I might:

  • 👋 Wave
  • 😊 Smile
  • 💬 Say "Hi!"
  • 💬 Say "Good morning!"
  • 👍 Nod or give a thumbs-up

I usually do not hug or cuddle with people in my Orange Circle.

I give them more personal space than family or close friends.
