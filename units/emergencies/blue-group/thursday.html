🚨 Thursday: What To Do During An Emergency

Today we will practice what to do when something unexpected happens.

⭐ My Emergency Plan

During an emergency, I can remember:

1️⃣ STOP

Stop what I am doing and pay attention.

2️⃣ LISTEN

Listen to adults and directions.

3️⃣ FOLLOW DIRECTIONS

Do what the teacher or helper says.

4️⃣ GET HELP

Tell a trusted adult what happened.

🎮 What Should I Do?

🔥 You smell smoke in the classroom.

🚨 The teacher gives directions during an emergency.

😟 You feel scared during an emergency.
