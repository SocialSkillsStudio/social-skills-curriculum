⭐ Who Helps Us?

Many people work together to keep us safe.

  • 🚒 Firefighters help during fires and rescues.
  • 🚑 Paramedics help people who are hurt or sick.
  • 👮 Police officers help keep people safe.
  • 👩‍🏫 Teachers and trusted adults help us follow safety plans.
