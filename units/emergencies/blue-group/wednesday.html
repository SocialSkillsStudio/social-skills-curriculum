🏫 Wednesday: School Safety & Drills

Today we will learn why schools practice safety drills and how we can make safe choices.

⭐ Why Do We Practice Drills?

Schools practice drills so everyone knows what to do during an emergency.

  • 👂 Listen to the teacher
  • 🚶 Walk safely
  • 🤫 Use a quiet voice
  • 🙋 Ask for help if needed
