🚨 Monday: What Is An Emergency?

Today we will learn what an emergency is and how to know when we need help.

⭐ What Is An Emergency?

An emergency is a situation where someone needs help right away.

Emergencies can feel scary, but we can stay safe by:

  • 🛑 Stopping our body
  • 👂 Listening carefully
  • 🚶 Following directions
  • 🙋 Asking for help
