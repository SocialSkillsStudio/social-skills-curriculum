🚨 Monday: What Is An Emergency?

Today we will learn what an emergency is and how to get help.

💭 Let's Learn

An emergency is something dangerous where someone needs help.

Examples of emergencies:

  • 🔥 A fire
  • 🚑 Someone is hurt
  • 🚗 A car accident
